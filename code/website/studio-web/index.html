How these examples relate to the research

Our question: does fine-tuning improve garment reconstruction while preserving the rest of the image? In the latest development validation, the 1,000-case model reduced average garment error by 6.8% and outside-clothing error by 14.7% compared with our earlier 63-case model, across 56 complete cases. Garment error still worsened on 23 of those cases.

This gallery is a separate visual audit of all 48 selected studio combinations at 768 × 1024. The controlled validation used different cases at 384 × 512 and two seeds. Neither average metric gains nor an attractive demo prove correct fit or reliable results for every photo. Colour, garment structure and preservation failures remain possible.
